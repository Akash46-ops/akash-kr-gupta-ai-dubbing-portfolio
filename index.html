Profile

From subtitles to supervising the dub.

I started as an English–Hindi translator and subtitler, and today I run a 10-person dubbing team at Media Shippers. I know the whole chain first-hand, from translating the script to lip-syncing the dialogue and mastering the final mix. Most of my work now is AI-assisted dubbing, where the real job is making sure the dub still sounds like the original actor. I enjoy the client side too: taking revision notes, turning them around quickly and walking people through what we delivered.

Workflow

How I take a dub from script to master

Translate → adapt → voice → sync → mix → master. The same five stages behind the samples above.

  1. 01
    Scene and language inAny format, any length, plus the target language.
  2. 02
    Transcribe and adaptLines rewritten to fit the mouth, the timing and the culture.
  3. 03
    Cast and shape the voicesThe AI voice keeps the actor's tone; every emotional beat is directed by a person.
  4. 04
    Mix and quality checkI listen through for lip-sync, levels, pronunciation, pacing and emotion.
  5. 05
    Deliver to specWAV audio, SRT, or MP4 with burnt-in subtitles, ready to upload.
